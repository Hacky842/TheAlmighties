Take a screenshot and send it (Windows)

  1. Press Win + Shift + S. The screen dims.
  2. Drag with the mouse over the area you want. It is copied automatically.
  3. Come back here and press Ctrl + V. The screenshot is attached.
  4. Press Ctrl + Enter to send.
Full screen instead? Press PrtScn, then Ctrl + V here. Want it saved as a file? Win + PrtScn saves it in Pictures → Screenshots.
